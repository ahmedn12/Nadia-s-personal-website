Career timeline

Jun 2025 – Present Charles River Laboratories Ashland, Ohio Current
Research Scientist I, Method Development
  • Lead regulated LC-MS/MS bioanalytical method development and qualification for small and large molecule programs across plasma, serum, tissue, and whole blood matrices, supporting 12+ client-sponsored projects.
  • Collaborate with study directors, validation teams, and cross-functional stakeholders to design fit-for-purpose quantitative bioanalytical assays aligned with FDA, EMA, and ICH expectations.
  • Develop and optimize sample extraction strategies (PPT, SPE, LLE, affinity capture) to maximize assay sensitivity, recovery, robustness, and reproducibility.
  • Author method development reports, SOPs, and technical documentation supporting assay transfer, qualification, and validation in compliance with ALCOA+ data integrity standards.
Nov 2024 – May 2025 Food and Drug Administration (FDA/CDER) St. Louis, Missouri
ORISE Fellow
  • Developed high-resolution LC-MS/MS and ion mobility (HRIM-MS, Cyclic IMS-MS) methods for diastereomeric separation and characterization of synthetic oligonucleotides.
  • Conducted impurity profiling, structural elucidation, and degradation product assessment of oligonucleotide drug substances and products.
  • Authored method development reports and technical documentation following FDA quality and data integrity standards; collaborated with regulatory scientists to translate outcomes into practical analytical workflows.
Aug 2020 – Nov 2024 Virginia Commonwealth University Richmond, Virginia
Graduate Assistant (RA & TA) — Dept. of Pharmaceutics
  • Led LC-MS/MS-based quantitative proteomic analysis of macrophages using Orbitrap Fusion Lumos and Q Exactive HFX, identifying key protein markers linked to lung inflammation.
  • Conducted peptide mapping, PTM analysis, and quantitative proteomics; applied bioinformatics and statistical approaches for pathway and genetic analysis.
  • Led projects on radioimmunotherapy for GBM and RNAi-based ADAR gene modulation for cancer immunotherapy, contributing to an NIH R35 grant proposal.
  • Designed “PESC 709: Pharmaceutical Engineering Lab II” course; developed syllabus, graded papers, and delivered 4 lectures for 12 graduate students.
May 2024 – Sep 2024 Food and Drug Administration (FDA/CDER) St. Louis, Missouri
Summer ORISE Fellow
  • Developed and validated a microchip-based CE–MS (ZipChip) method for high-throughput quantitative impurity analysis of synthetic oligonucleotides.
  • Authored SOPs and validation summaries; presented findings to FDA research and regulatory teams emphasizing applicability for routine QC and lot-release testing.
  • Completed FDA 101 training and a 2-month PBPK Modeling workshop (Dr. Raymond Yang).
Dec 2019 – May 2020 University of South Florida Tampa, Florida
Research Assistant — Dept. of Internal Medicine
  • Developed and optimized lipid-based nanocarriers for targeted intranasal delivery of lapatinib and ketoconazole; characterized nanoparticles and conducted in vitro drug release and cell uptake studies.
  • Contributed to “Preclinical efficacy of TN-1008” — anticancer drug screening, PCR, flow cytometry for gene expression, cell viability, and stemness markers.
Jul 2017 – Aug 2018 Radiant Pharmaceuticals Ltd. Tongi, Bangladesh
Development Pharmacist
  • Led formulation development and analytical method development for tablets and oral liquid dosage forms per USP, BP, and ICH guidelines under cGMP & ISO standards.
